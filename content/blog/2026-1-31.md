It is hard to cope with the knowledge that every one I know and care about is being hurt by a system that I don't see a path forward to solve right now. Capitalism is destroying earth, it is working us to death and it is taking our time. It is easy to be down about the world. It is a system that is hurting everyone so that there should be more people trying to change it but people seem to just accept the world as it is. Rage and sheer strength of will won't solve this but it is important to never give up. I think we must convince more people that the world is not doomed to be this way. People, more than ever before, are capable of changing the world.
